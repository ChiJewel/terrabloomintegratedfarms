About TerraBloom

An International Agribusiness Built for Modern Africa

TerraBloom Integrated Farms was established with a singular corporate conviction: to elevate Nigerian agriculture to global industrial standards through precision technology, sustainable stewardship, and integrated value chains.

High-Tech Greenhouses

Climate-controlled poly-houses shielding high-value vegetables from pests, extreme heat, and tropical downpours.

Certified Agronomy

Pure hybrid seeds and certified veterinary bloodlines ensuring optimal yield, nutritional density, and food safety.

Zero-Waste Bio-Loop

Poultry & cattle manure is converted into organic fertilizer for crops; crop residues are formulated into animal silage.

Community AgTech Hub

Empowering local youth and women farmers with technical modern agronomy training and outgrower contracts.

Corporate ESG Standards

Smart Technology & Environmental Responsibility

Operating in alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 2: Zero Hunger, SDG 12: Responsible Consumption).

Precision Drip Irrigation

Automated soil moisture sensors deliver micro-doses of water directly to plant roots, eliminating water wastage by up to 65% compared to conventional furrow farming.

Solar Hybrid Energy Grid

Over 600 kW of rooftop solar arrays power our cold storage rooms, borehole pumping networks, and automated poultry ventilations, sharply reducing carbon footprint.

Circular Bio-Economy

Poultry litter and livestock manure undergo thermo-biological composting into pathogen-free organic fertilizer pellets, restoring soil organic matter naturally.

Local Community Empowerment

Over 70% of our on-site workforce comprises talent from Ogun and Lagos border communities, supported by full healthcare, fair wages, and AgTech apprenticeship programs.
